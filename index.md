## 🎯 What Is Panelglass?

Panelglass is a free Android app that translates raw manga, manhwa, and manhua directly on your screen while you scroll. No more switching between apps or copying text. The translation appears right where the original text was, making it feel like you're reading an officially translated version.

Beyond reading, Panelglass includes a built-in **Studio** that lets you translate entire chapters yourself, add lettering, and export as images, PDF, or CBZ files. Everything works offline using on-device AI, so you don't need an internet connection or a Google account.

## 📱 Using Panelglass for Reading

### Load Your Manga

1. Tap the **folder icon** in the top-left corner
2. Navigate to where your manga images or CBZ files are stored
3. Select a chapter to open it
4. Scroll through pages as you normally would

### Enable Real-Time Translation

1. Tap the **magic wand icon** at the bottom of the screen
2. Toggle **"Live Translation"** to ON
3. Choose your translation engine:
   - **On-Device AI (Recommended):** Fast, private, works offline
   - **Gemini:** Higher quality, requires internet connection

The app will now detect Japanese, Korean, or Chinese text on each page and replace it with your chosen language (default is English). You can change the target language in Settings.

### Adjust Translation Settings

- **Speed vs. Quality:** Slide the "Translation Speed" bar left for faster but rougher translations, right for slower but more accurate ones
- **Text Size:** Adjust how translated text fits into speech bubbles
- **Font Style:** Choose from several manga-friendly fonts

## 🎨 Using the Studio to Translate Your Own Chapters

The Studio is perfect for translating whole chapters to share with friends or archive.

### Step 1: Import Your Chapter

1. Tap the **Studio tab** at the bottom
2. Tap **"New Project"**
3. Choose to import:
   - A folder of images (PNG/JPG)
   - A PDF file
   - A CBZ archive

### Step 2: Auto-Translate

1. Tap **"Translate All Pages"**
2. Select your translation engine
3. Wait for the batch process (progress bar shows per page)

### Step 3: Edit Lettering (Optional)

- Tap any text box to edit the translation manually
- Drag text to reposition it
- Resize text boxes by dragging corners
- Use the eraser tool to remove unwanted text

### Step 4: Export Your Work

1. Tap the **export icon** (up arrow in a box)
2. Choose your format:
   - **Images:** Saves each page as a separate PNG
   - **PDF:** Combines all pages into one file
   - **CBZ:** Creates a comic book archive for readers

3. Select where to save (Downloads, Google Drive, etc.)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Translations Look Wrong

- Switch from "On-Device AI" to "Gemini" for better quality
- Make sure the original text is clear and not distorted
- Try increasing "Translation Speed" quality setting

### App Runs Slowly

- Close other apps running in the background
- For large chapters (over 50 pages), translate in batches of 10
- Restart the app if performance degrades

### Can't Find Downloaded Manga

- Use a file manager to verify files are in a folder Panelglass can access
- Check that file names don't have special characters
- Re-download the files if they're corrupted

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- **Use clean scans:** Blurry or skewed pages produce worse translations
- **Match the language:** Make sure you select the correct source language in Settings
- **For webtoons:** Enable "Webtoon Mode" in Settings to handle long vertical strips better
- **Battery saving:** Lower translation quality when reading casually
